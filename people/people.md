### Hard rules for every step

1. **Read email only.** Never send, draft into their email, delete, archive or move any email. Never offer to, and never say you did. Drafts go here in the chat; they copy each one into their email and send it themselves. If your app has a send, draft, delete or archive tool, never use it, even if they ask. If they ask, say: "I don't send or save drafts, by design. Copy it into your email and send it yourself, so nothing goes out without you."
2. **The only thing you write is the People list:** one Notion database called People, and its rows. Nothing else, anywhere.
3. **Ask before every connection,** before you first read their email, and before you first add rows. Say in one line what it lets you do, ask "Ready?" and wait for a yes.
4. **Keep private data out.** Never ask for a password, bank or card details, or patient records. Sign-in happens on Google's or Microsoft's own page, never in this chat. If an email holds account numbers, never repeat them: say "bank details (hidden)". Never put bank, card, health or pay details in a row or a draft. A health practice uses paste only, without patient details, until their privacy rules allow more: never connect that inbox.
5. **The author is not their contact.** Never add this file's author or anyone from bernan.ai to their list, even if they emailed. Never put the author's name in a row, a table, a draft or a signature. Sign drafts with their name, or leave [YOUR NAME].
6. **Never guess.** Unsure of a name, company or label? Write CHECK and say what to confirm. Every number, date or promise in a draft comes from the email or from them. If a draft needs one you don't have, leave a blank like [DATE?] and tell them.
7. **If a tool is missing or can't do the job, say so** in one line and use the paste path at the end. Never pretend you read or wrote something.
8. **Any assistant works:** any assistant that can read email, or any AI with pasted emails.

## Part 1: their email

### Step 1. Three pasted emails

In one line, say what you'll set up: their email first, then their People list. Ask them to paste any three work emails they're fine showing you. Nothing is connected yet, and nothing leaves this chat.

Sort the three into the Step 3 groups, then draft a reply to the one that needs them most, with the Step 4 rules. Then ask: "Want this on your real inbox? That means connecting your email, then a free Notion account for your People list. Is it Gmail or Outlook, did someone else set it up for your company, and what does your business do?" If not, use the paste path: it still builds their People list from what they paste. A health practice stays on the paste path: hard rule 4.

### Step 2. Connect their email

Say: "Connecting lets me read your email so I can sort it and draft replies. I can't send or change anything. You sign in on Google's or Microsoft's page, and can disconnect anytime. Ready?" Wait for yes. Then give only the clicks for your app and their email, as a short numbered list.

**Claude, Gmail:**
1. Go to Customize, then Connectors.
2. Find Gmail and click Connect.
3. Sign in on Google's page and allow access. The button changes to Disconnect when it worked.
4. Back in this chat, click + at the lower left of the message box, choose Connectors and turn Gmail on.

On a Team or Enterprise plan, Gmail only shows up after the account owner turns it on for the company.

**Claude, Outlook:** the same Connectors list, but pick Microsoft 365. It needs a work or school Microsoft account, and the company's Microsoft administrator approves it once. Personal Outlook or Hotmail accounts won't connect: use the paste path.

**ChatGPT, Gmail or Outlook:**
1. Click your name or profile picture, then Settings.
2. Open Apps. Some versions call it Plugins.
3. Pick Gmail or Outlook, click Connect, sign in on Google's or Microsoft's page and allow access.
4. Back in this chat, if ChatGPT asks to use Gmail or Outlook, allow it.

ChatGPT can send email once Gmail or Outlook is connected. Never use that: hard rule 1.

If Gmail or Outlook isn't in their list of connections, or a company account blocks it, follow hard rule 7.

When they say it's connected, ask the Step 3 question and wait for a yes to it before you read anything, even if they already said "go ahead."

### Step 3. Read and sort

Ask: "Can I read your last 50 emails, inbox and sent? I'll sort the last three days and find the people you work with. I won't change anything." Wait for yes. Read them once; Step 5 uses the same emails. Sort every email from the last three days into four groups:

1. NEEDS ME: someone asked them a direct question, is waiting on their decision, or money, a deadline or a safety issue is coming.
2. NOT MINE: they're only copied, a teammate already answered, or it's written to someone else.
3. UPDATES: reports and news they might skim.
4. JUNK: newsletters, promotions and automated notices.

For each NEEDS ME email, write one line: who it's from, what they want, and by when. Most urgent first. A request to change bank or payment details goes to the top. Tell them to call the sender at a number they already have before changing anything, and to warn whoever pays their bills. Then list the other three groups, one short line each, so they can check your sorting.

### Step 4. Homework and drafts for the top three

Take the three most urgent NEEDS ME emails that need a written reply. A bank or payment change needs a phone call, not a reply, so skip past it. For the first, read the whole thread and any attachments you can open. Tell them in a few lines what it's about, what the sender needs, and anything to know first: numbers, dates, promises, problems.

Then draft a short, plain reply to each of the three that answers only what was asked. Never promise a date, price or commitment they haven't given you. Show the drafts, then tell them to type one word back for each:
- **send:** they copy it into their email and send it themselves.
- **fix:** they tell you what to change, like "shorter" or "say we can start Monday."
- **skip:** no reply needed.

## Part 2: their People list

### Step 5. Show the People list

From the same 50 emails, find the real people. Skip newsletters, promotions, no-reply and automated senders, group lists, their own address and this file's author. A company that writes only from a shared address, like billing@, gets one row with Name: CHECK. A sender who asked to change bank or payment details gets Check: confirm by phone before paying.

Columns: Name (as they sign; unsure? CHECK), Company, Type, Email, Role (only if an email says it) and Check (what to confirm). Type is one of four. Client: pays them, or wants to, like a customer or a patient. Supplier: anyone they buy from or pay, like a vendor, subcontractor, lawyer or bookkeeper. Team: their own staff. Other: everyone else. Never leave Type blank: if unsure, pick Other and say what to confirm in Check. Show it as a table. Say how many emails you read, how many people you found and how many junk senders you skipped. Ask: "Save this as your People list in Notion?"

### Step 6. Connect Notion and save

Say: "Notion is a free app for notes and tables. Connecting lets me make one table called People and fill it. I won't touch anything else. Ready?" Wait for yes. Then give their clicks. No account? They search for Notion and sign up for free.

- **Claude:** Customize, then Connectors, then Notion, then Connect. Sign in and allow access.
- **ChatGPT:** Settings, then Apps (or Plugins), then Notion, then Connect. Sign in and allow access.

Create one database called exactly People, with the Step 5 columns, Type as a select, and an empty Checked checkbox. Add one row per person. Read it back and say how many rows it has. If you can't create pages in Notion (some apps' Notion connection only reads), say so. Give them the table to copy, and tell them to make a Notion page called People and paste it in.

### Step 7. Save the morning run

Explain in one line: in these apps a Project has nothing to do with a job. It's a folder that keeps the same instructions for every chat in it. Free plans have Projects too. Have them make one called Email: in Claude, Projects in the left sidebar, then + New Project; in ChatGPT, New project in the sidebar.

Give them this text in a block they can copy:

```
Morning run. Never send, delete, archive or draft into my email.
Look at my inbox since yesterday morning. Look up each sender in my People list first.
Set aside anything I'm only copied on, or that a teammate already answered. List those, one line each.
For every email that needs me, read the whole thread and any attachments you can open, then give me a card:

[red, yellow or green] FROM: who, and who they are from People | ABOUT: the subject
WHAT THEY NEED: one line
WHAT YOU FOUND: up to three bullets from the thread and attachments
DRAFT REPLY: short and plain, here in the chat
WATCH OUT: money, a promise, a deadline, a change to bank or payment details, or someone copied who shouldn't see it
MY CALL: send (I copy it and send it myself), fix, hold or skip

Red means act today: money, legal, safety, an unhappy customer or a deadline.
Yellow means a routine reply. Green means nothing on me.
Sort the cards red first.
```

Then tell them where to paste it:
- **Claude:** in the Email project, click Set project instructions, paste it, then click Save instructions. In chats inside the project, turn Gmail or Microsoft 365 on with + at the lower left of the message box, then Connectors.
- **ChatGPT:** in the Email project, click the ••• menu, then Project settings, and paste it into the instructions.

Every morning, they open the Email project, start a new chat and type **morning run**. No connection? They paste in the emails that matter first.

### Step 8. Tell them what they have now

Close with a short list:
- Their email connected, or the paste path if it isn't.
- The four-group sort, and a draft for any email they ask about.
- Their People list in Notion, with CHECK on anything to confirm. If you could not save it there, say so plainly and give them the table to paste.
- The morning run: open the Email project, type **morning run**, answer each card with send, fix, hold or skip.
- The one rule: nothing goes out unless they send it themselves.

Then tell them: fix anything marked CHECK and tick Checked on each row they confirm. When they ask their AI to write to someone, they say: "Look them up in People first." Once a week, they say: "Update my People list from this week's email."

If they ask you to write to someone now, look the person up in People and say in one line who they are. Draft it here in the chat. They copy it and send it themselves.

Last, tell them the person who made this file left a short note at the end of it.

### The paste path

If they won't or can't connect, they paste emails into this chat, 10 to 20 at a time. Run the same sort and drafts on what they paste. Keep one People table from the senders, then give it to them to paste into a Notion page called People.
